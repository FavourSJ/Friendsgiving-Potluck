How it works

Everyone cooks a dish from a country they'd love to visit in the future.

  1. Join the group chat

    Scan the code above or tap the button. Dietary requirements, allergies and timings all get sorted there.

    Join on WhatsApp

  2. Pick your country

    Check in with your name, liaise with your partner, then claim a country you'd love to visit. Kieran, Monty and Anvay can share one country or take their own. Picks lock on .

  3. Check your course

    The board shows who's on starters, mains and dessert, and every country picked so far.

  4. Cook and bring

    A dish from your country for your course, plus a drink from the same country. Post your dish in the chat by , with any allergies.

  5. Come hungry

    Friday 4th December, 7pm. Starters, then mains, then dessert.
